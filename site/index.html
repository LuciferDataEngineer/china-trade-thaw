The Australian dollar bought 0.70 US dollars on average in 2019 and 0.64 in 2025, so it was 7 percent weaker. On the latest published day it bought 0.70 US dollars and 4.77 yuan. Export income on these pages is in Australian dollars, the way the ABS publishes it.

What this means

China's share was 12% in 2006 and 38.0% in 2019. It fell to 29.1% in 2022, when coal sales stopped, and was 33.6% in 2025. A 10 percent fall in sales to China would put $19.2 billion of export income at stake in a year like the last one. In 2025, iron ore alone was $103.6 billion, 59% of everything sold to China.
