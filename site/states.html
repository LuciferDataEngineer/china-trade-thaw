What this means

Western Australia sent 57.1% of its goods exports to China in October 2025 to September 2026. Tasmania's share was 32%, but the income at stake there is $173 million. The map shows the share. The bars show the dollars.

Goods exports are published by the Australian Bureau of Statistics, merchandise trade, free under CC BY 4.0. Exchange rates are the Reserve Bank of Australia's public tables. State outlines are Natural Earth, public domain. China here excludes Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan, which are published separately. Figures were taken on 21 November 2026, 9:37 AM AEDT, Australia/Melbourne time. Dollars are Australian dollars, not adjusted for inflation.
